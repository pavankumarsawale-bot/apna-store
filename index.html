📲 Install Sawale World Store App

Offline Store App: You can install this application on your Windows PC / Laptop or Mobile Phone for instant 1-click access anytime!

🖥️ Method 1: Install on Windows PC / Laptop (PWA App)

  1. In Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, look at the right side of the address bar at the top.
  2. Click the "Install app" icon (looks like a monitor with an arrow 🖥️⬇️ or a (+) plus icon).
  3. Click "Install".
  4. A Sawale World Store app icon is instantly created on your Desktop & Taskbar!

⚡ Method 2: Create Windows Desktop Icon (10 seconds)

  1. Open your Desktop\l folder.
  2. Right-click on Start_Sawale_Store.bat.
  3. Click "Send to" → "Desktop (create shortcut)".
  4. Rename the shortcut on your desktop to "Sawale World Store". Done!

📱 Method 3: Install on Android Phone / Tablet

  1. Open the store app on your mobile phone's Chrome browser.
  2. Tap the 3 vertical dots (⋮) menu at top right.
  3. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. The app icon will appear on your phone screen and open in full screen!
